The format

You manage a fantasy squad of real {{ rules.league.competition }} players. Each round your team is scored on what your players actually did on the pitch, and you play a head-to-head match against another manager in the league.

The draft

Squads are built in a live snake draft before the season starts. Every player is owned by exactly one manager - there is no salary cap and no duplicate ownership.

  • Snake order. Round one runs first-to-last, round two runs last-to-first, and it keeps alternating. Picking last means picking first next time, so no one is stuck at the back of every round.
  • {{ dr.picks_per_team }} picks each. {% if dr.optional_fr_pick %} That's {{ sq.squad_size }} individuals plus one pick you may spend on a club front-row unit. Skip the unit and you simply draft an extra individual. {% else %} One for every place in the matchday {{ sq.squad_size }}. {% endif %}
  • {{ dr.pick_seconds }} seconds per pick. Let the clock run out and you're auto-drafted: the system fills your most urgent unfilled position first, then takes the best player available.
  • The commissioner sets the draft order and date. The draft must be scheduled to finish before the first kickoff of round 1.
  • Draft rankings are last season's total points, matched by player name. Newcomers with no history start at zero - rank is a guide, not a valuation.

When the draft ends your squad is placed automatically: the highest-ranked player in each position starts, the rest go to the bench. Rearrange it on the Squad page before the deadline.

How points are scored

Player points come from the official fantasy stats for {{ rules.league.competition }}. Those totals are cumulative across the season, so your score for a round is the player's total after the round minus their total before it - what they earned that week, and nothing else.

  • Kicking points always count. There's no designated goal kicker to nominate.
    • {% if sc.captain %}
  • Captain scores double. Name one captain each round and their round points are multiplied by {{ sc.captain_multiplier }}. Pick a captain who is actually starting - see auto-subs below.
    • {% endif %} {% if sc.fr_unit %}
  • The front-row unit scores the combined points of that club's props and hookers who were named in the real matchday squad that round. If no lineup has been published yet, all of the club's front-rowers count.

Before real line-ups are published, your named starters score as picked.

Deadlines & lockout

Your team locks at the first kickoff of the round. Not a fixed day or time - the actual first whistle of the round's earliest real match.

  • Once locked, you can't change your line-up{% if sc.captain %}, your captain,{% endif %} or make any transfer for that round.
  • Picks reopen when the round's last match finishes, and you build the next round's team.
  • If you don't change anything, your team carries forward - you're never left with an empty side.
  • Kickoff times come from the official fixture list. Some later rounds are published as provisional slots until the league confirms them, so check the Fixtures page rather than assuming a usual kickoff time.

Transfers & trades

Squads change in two ways: pick up an unowned player, or trade with another manager. Both are locked as soon as the round kicks off.

Free agents

  • Any player no one owns is a free agent. Pick one up by dropping one of your own players - always one in, one out, so your squad size never changes.
  • Free-agent moves are immediate. There's no waiver period and no bidding - first in gets the player.
  • The incoming player inherits the slot of the player they replaced (starter or bench).

Trades with other managers

  • One-for-one only. Propose a swap; it happens only if the other manager accepts.
  • Either side can cancel or decline while the offer is still pending.
    • {% if sq.strict %}
  • Like-for-like. Both players must be the same position - a lock for a lock. This keeps every squad legal, since the matchday {{ sq.squad_size }} has no spare slots to absorb a mismatch.
    • {% endif %} {% if sq.fr_unit %}
  • Front-row units are tradeable as a single asset - for another club's unit, or for an individual player. You can only ever hold one, so drop yours before picking up another.
  • Individual {{ sq.fr_positions|join(' and ') }} can't be traded - they belong to the club unit, not to you.
    • {% endif %}

Finals

After round {{ se.regular_rounds }} the table seeds two brackets, and both run two-legged semi-finals in rounds {{ se.semi_leg1 }} and {{ se.semi_leg2 }} with the final in round {{ se.final_round }}.

Championship

  • The top 4 qualify.
  • Semi-finals are 1 v 4 and 2 v 3.
  • Both legs are added together - highest aggregate advances.
  • Level on aggregate? The higher seed goes through.
  • Win the final and you're champion.

Sacko

  • The bottom 4 are dragged in.
  • It's inverted: losing advances you. Win and you escape.
  • Needs at least {{ se.sacko_min_teams }} teams in the league to run.
  • Lose the final and the wooden spoon is yours - and it goes on your record permanently.

Championships and sackos are attached to your account, not your team name. Rename your team, or come back next season, and your honours follow you.
